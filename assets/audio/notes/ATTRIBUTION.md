This app uses derived mono WAV clips generated from these source samples:
- C4v6.flac
- D#4v6.flac
- F#4v6.flac
- A4v6.flac

The derived clips are trimmed, pitch-shifted, loudness-matched, and exported
to `c4.wav` through `b4.wav` by `tools/generate_piano_samples.py`.
